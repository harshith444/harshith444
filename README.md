## AI Data Lab

I build data products that do more than report numbers.

My work sits at the intersection of analytics engineering, AI workflows, and scalable data systems. I turn scattered data into intelligent pipelines, decision-ready dashboards, automation layers, and machine-learning-ready foundations that help teams move faster.

Right now, I am exploring AI-powered data projects that combine analytics, automation, and machine learning to create systems that can explain, recommend, and improve.

```yaml

  - machine-learning-ready datasets
  - decision intelligence systems

  - automated data quality and anomaly detection pipelines
  - analytics layers that convert raw events into business-ready signals
```
